## 3. The corrector stack (in order)
1. `correct_tip` — CNN peak-snap (20px confirm, sub-pixel refine) → live-curve endpoint fallback (5–25px, capped) → far-candidate tier (45–110px, flagged, never trusted directly). Fault samples refuse entirely (`at_fault`). Anchor capped at 45px (uncapped anchors validated 80–103px foreign hijacks).
2. `consensus_smooth` — median-of-5 neighboring fixes, veto jumps >12px. Runs EXACTLY ONCE (re-running vetoes genuine era shifts).
3. `sticky_bias` — hysteresis bias tracker (adopt on ≥4-agreeing fixes, ramp over 6, hold otherwise; fault/veto samples RESET). Candidates join agreement windows only.
4. `build_sticky_series.py` — the unified tool; `--keep-col/--vetoed-col` respect upstream decisions. Both ships reproduce bit-identically.

## 4. Training program (all dead ends kept)
- v1 (production, val 0.01254): chunked training with 3 SIGTERM restarts via `--init-checkpoint`.
- v2 (corners): corner-anchored negatives killed faints (P3 4→135). v2r (relabel-only): flipped P11 (10.8→37.8), zero corner recall. v3a (wide-sigma faint labels): P3 4.3→135 — tolerating blur teaches blur.
- v4valid (validity ignore-discs, 2,217 weak tips mapped): first retrain to spare P11 (12.8) but P3 still 4.3→135.3 — continued training boosts bright-channel gain; faint argmax flips.
- v5human (487 audited human labels ONLY): STARVED — P13-clean 4.3→192, peak counts halved. Weak labels are load-bearing as data regardless of target quality.
- v3human (this round: weak labels KEPT + 31 human tips + 24 tip-negative frames, warm-started, 10ep): gate FAIL, P3 4.3→133.7. Mechanism per H230: P3's margin is a coin-flip every continued training perturbs — not starvation, perturbation. P11 held 10.8; ghosts stayed silent.
- Standing pattern (4/4 retrains): continued training degrades the faint class. Next attempt needs a frozen backbone + head (or equivalent perturbation-free design), not more epochs. No retrains queued.
- Gate rule (enforced in code, exit 1 = no promotion): any CONFIRMED→miss flip (dist crossing 20px up) or silent→fire flip (heat crossing 0.10 up) FAILS. Note: evaluate on `probes_v3.csv` (11 probes); `/tmp/rerun_probes.csv` predates P8-apex + pre-emergence probes.

## 6. Negatives with mechanisms (do not retry without new info)
- Visibility covariate: |t|=1.1, burst split → retired. Survives as tip-freshness descriptor only.
- Optical flow: weak detector, wrong-way on corners.
- Frangi/ridge tracing: tubes below coherence scale; apex vesselness = 0.
- TTA (CLAHE/stretch/upscale): all hurt; net is contrast-calibrated.
- Damped extrapolation, terminus preference, masked root support (circle over-masks), size/angular gates, whole-path phantom rule: all reverted with cause in LEDGER.
- RMSE-vs-self as promotion metric: rewards laggy smoothness. Step-plausibility instead.
- Corner/kink bound: no extrapolation from one side of a kink foresees the other — information-theoretic. Corners contained, never fixed.
- Joint grain+tip multi-task training: 18:1 label imbalance lets grain hijack the backbone (negative transfer).
