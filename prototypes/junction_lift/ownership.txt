Ownership fixes at junctions (4 Oct 2026; branch junction-ownership; SparseTrack 0.8.8 = base088)
=================================================================================================

Two options from results.txt's conclusion, each off by default (commit fdbfb86, tests/test_flood_ownership.py):

1. Params.flood_disc_pass (learned.disc_passages; flood_disc_width 12): a flooded tube may cross another census
   grain's blocked disc (r + 2 px) along a band where the map shows material entering and leaving the disc (edge
   points >= 60 deg apart round it, <= 120 deg of its edge covered, <= 12 px wide), never the grain's own body, rim
   or a tube starting from it.
2. Params.flood_pass_bins (flood pass_bins = 6; flood_pass_px 20, flood_pass_reach 15): a start is on probation
   for 6 bins; a tube that by then spreads more than 20 px along the rim while reaching less than 15 px out is
   another tube passing the rim: forgotten, its material (and pieces touching it) never a start, and the flood keeps
   looking for the grain's own start.

What the cases look like (disc_diag.py, disc_open_all.py, start_diag.py, start_evo.py, wrong_starts.py)

   Discs. The m2 traces that run through another grain's disc do not cross it as a narrow band on the map:
   g061's disc (g052) is 98% marked as tube at some time (one blob 21 px wide over 340 deg of its edge), g041's
   (g038) 54%, g068's (g092) 77%, g065's (g064) 79%; g037's (g005) shows the tube only from bin 337. And inside
   the disc the true route crosses material that arrived before the tube got there (g052: arrivals 100-129 inside,
   the tube reaches the disc at bin 167), i.e. an older crossing, which the arrival flood does not claim. Opening
   the whole discs (no band test; diagnostic only) changes one trace: g038@349 58 -> 68 px (human 157).

   Starts. At its start bin the flood's starting piece is small and on its own in every case looked at (1-48 px,
   nothing older joined to it), so "already long / far end first / both ways past the grain" cannot be seen then;
   a passing tube shows in the next bins as fast spread along the rim with little reach out (m1 g013: 19 -> 55 px
   along the rim in 6 bins, 7 px out; m1 g065: 3 -> 24 px, 13 px out), where grains' own young tubes spread
   <= 9 px in their first 6 bins. m2 g038's start was right (its first two traces are hits); it goes wrong later
   at a crossing, and its traces at bins 244 and 349 start inside its census circle (the grain moved; its drift was
   rejected), so it is read where the grain no longer is. Starts > 45 deg round the grain
   from the human exit at the first full trace: m2 2/18 flood-read grains (g048, a doubtful trace; g064, a 5 px
   stub), m1 3/26 (g013, g065, g068 a 1 px reading), ld 1/5.

Conclusion: neither fix moves lengths. The disc rule as specified never fires on m2's cases (the discs are
blobs of tube material, not crossed by a band) and opening discs outright barely matters, because inside them the
route crosses older material. The start probation fixes one m1 start (onset +1) and removes one wrong tube without
finding the right one. Both stay off. The common thread in all the junction cases is arrival order: material that
was there first (older crossings, tubes over grains) or arrives elsewhere (moved tubes and grains) is invisible to a
flood that claims only what arrives at its tip.
